# Subcult Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026. This policy covers the Subcult iPhone app (currently in TestFlight beta) and the Subcult service it uses.

**Account.** If you sign in, we store your email address, handle and profile to run your account. Sign-in codes are sent by email.

**Your activity.** Records you keep, notes you write, collections you create and places you follow are stored with your account. Public collections and notes are visible to others.

**Listening without an account.** Nothing about you is stored on our servers. The app keeps local preferences on your device.

**Identify.** When you tap Identify, the microphone listens briefly and Apple's ShazamKit matches the sound. We don't record or store the audio.

**Music sources.** Playback streams from third-party sources such as Bandcamp and Apple Music previews; those services receive standard network requests.

**No advertising or tracking.** No advertising, no tracking across apps, and no selling of data.

**Deleting your data.** Email [ak@subcult.music](mailto:ak@subcult.music) to delete your account or data. In-app deletion is coming.

**Contact.** [ak@subcult.music](mailto:ak@subcult.music)
